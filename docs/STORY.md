# PLATFORM 0 — story bible (v3.2)

## One line
A young man keeps trying to catch the last local home to his mother. He has been trying for ten years.

## Who you are
**Arjun Desai, 23.** Night-shift trainee at a call centre near Shunya Road junction. Red backpack, headphones always in.
Lives with his widowed mother **Kamla Desai — "Maa"** (married Ramesh in 1989: the "K + R · 1989" bangle; she has worn
widow's white since). He loves her and is a little ashamed of how often he lets her calls ring out.

**What he wants:** to get home tonight. He promised: "home by 12:30".

## What really happened
**Sunday 30 Oct 2016.** Arjun misses the 11:41 again. Maa calls twice; he lets it ring (headphones). On the platform he plays her voicemail
("I'll stay up… until you come home.") and texts back "Home by 12:30". The message never sends. The board sends the 11:58 slow local to **Platform 0**, a platform closed for years.
The 11:58 is cancelled that night, so he paces up and down the platform with his music on. At **23:58** a goods train
runs through Platform 0. The driver sounds the horn twice. Arjun doesn't turn around.

**31 Oct 2016, 02:17.** The station calls Maa: "Aunty, please come." 2:17 is the minute his death became real to her.

**2016–2018.** Every night Maa takes the 11:58 to Shunya Road with his steel tiffin and sits on Platform 0 (her diary
pages). The chai uncle gives her tea and won't take money. He tells her he sees Arjun walking the platform, and that
Arjun never looks at him.

**31 Oct 2018, 02:17.** Every clock in the station stops. A local with no lights, the **0217 "Platform Zero Local"**,
comes in on Platform 0. It is in no timetable. Maa has bought a ticket: one way, one adult. She boards. Her things are
found on the platforms and end up in **Lost & Found**. Station master S. Mehta writes **Incident Report 0217** and sets
the door code of his office, **Room 0**, to the time "so I never forget".

**Every year since,** at 02:17 on 31 October, one more person who was *waiting* on Platform 0 is gone (the logbook).
**Shunya Road** ("zero road", शून्य) keeps people who are waiting for someone who isn't coming.

## Tonight
31 October, ten years on. The last line in the station master's logbook is in fresh ink: **YOU**.
Arjun doesn't know any of this. As far as he knows it is 11:43 PM, he just got off a train, and he has fifteen minutes.

## The loop
The station replays Arjun's last half hour every night and stitches it wrong. The **anomalies** are the station's lies;
noticing them and **turning back** is Arjun refusing to keep walking blind. Eight right calls and he sees a little more.
- **The man with the red backpack** you keep passing is Arjun himself: his last walk, still going.
- **Lost tickets** are Arjun's own notes, written each night and forgotten again.
- **Diary pages** are Maa's diary, two years of nights on Platform 0.
- **The payphone** is the station: sometimes it helps, sometimes it lies.

## The ghost
**The woman in the white saree is Maa.** She isn't hunting him; she is trying to reach him. She can only move when he
isn't looking, because in life he never looked back when she called. Here that has become a rule. He sees her the way
the station shows her: soaked, broken, terrifying. When she gets close he panics and the loop throws him back to the start.
Her diary says it plainly: "Every time I reach for him he screams and runs." In the end, the only thing to do is turn around.

## Chapters
1. **Platform 0** — get off Platform 0 by the foot-over-bridge. Phone buzzes, a voicemail, the chai uncle, the 2:17 clocks,
   Room 0.
2. **The Subway** — the bridge didn't lead out; it led down. Cross to Platform 1. The message to Maa says "not delivered".
   A voicemail arrives timed 02:17. The old man on the mat says Platform 0 has been closed since the accident.
3. **The Last Local** — the 11:58 is finally there. Get to the front of the train. Every passenger is asleep, the next
   station is always Shunya Road, and Maa's voice is very close.

## Endings
- **A · SEE YOU TOMORROW** (reach the end): dawn, 6:12 AM. The backpack man was you; the newspaper says how. The board
  reads NEXT: YOU · TOMORROW. You never turned around, so the night starts again.
- **B · MAA** (all 6 diary pages): at dawn she is there. "She was never chasing you." This time Arjun turns around and
  goes to her. She lets him go home, but she stays: her things are still in Lost & Found and her name is still in the
  logbook, so the station still holds her. "Jaa, beta. Ghar jaa. Main yahin hoon."
- **C · THE 0217 — TRUE ENDING** (6 pages + her 6 things + Room 0 opened): it is 2:17. You know the train is coming for
  you (your name is the last line) and you have given back everything the station kept of hers. The 0217 comes in on
  Platform 0 and they board it together: DEPARTED 02:17 · 2 PASSENGERS. The clocks start again at 2:18, and the next
  year nobody's name is added to the logbook.

## Tone
Quiet, rain, tube lights, PA chimes, ordinary Indian railway details turned slightly wrong. English with natural Hindi
(Devanagari for signs and a few lines). Everything skippable; story scenes play once, and replays stay fast.
