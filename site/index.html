CHAPTERS

Chapters unlock as you reach them. Lost tickets and diary pages you find are kept.

HOW TO PLAY

You're stuck in a loop. Every time you walk through, the place repeats.

  • If something is wrong (an anomaly), turn back the way you came.
  • If nothing is wrong, keep going forward.
  • Right call: the number goes up. Wrong call: back to 0.
  • Reach 8 to escape the chapter. There are three.
  • Your goal for each chapter is at the top of the screen (and in the pause menu).
  • Pick up the 8 lost tickets: Arjun's notes on what happened that night.
  • Some nights the lights die: grab the torch from the station master's room. Batteries run out.
  • If a phone rings, you can answer it. It might help you. It might not.
  • She only moves when you aren't looking at her. If she catches you, you lose health (the red bar, top left); it slowly comes back when things are quiet. At 0 you wake back at the start of the chapter (the count goes back to 0), but everything you found is kept.
  • Torch flare (phone: 🔆, PC: Q or right-click): a blinding burst that stuns her for a few seconds and throws her back. Costs battery and needs a few seconds to recharge.
  • Something is watching: after your first turn-back, someone with a lamp stands at the far end of Platform 0. It is always there now, and it is never an anomaly, there or not. Each time you turn back, it is a little closer.
  • It remembers you: where you last fell, there's a chalk outline (not an anomaly). Settings → Forget me erases it.
  • 📖 Archive: every anomaly you turn back on unlocks a note about what happened at Shunya Road.
  • The stranger: from the third loop on you may hear your own footsteps echo… then see someone who looks exactly like you, copying you. Don't walk at him. When he snaps, he runs. One catch takes a third of your health.
  • Fight back: the flare dissolves him. Pick up a steel rod or broken umbrella and swing it (PC: R or left-click, phone: ⚔️): 3 hits and he's gone, and it breaks after 5 swings. Up close you can shove him away (PC: Space, phone: ✋). Your hands go straight through her.
  • Hide under a bench, in the waiting-room corner, behind the subway shop or the box, or between train seats (USE). If he saw you go in, he drags you out.
  • In the coach the TC checks tickets: “tikat dikhao…” Show him a lost ticket and he moves on. Without one…
  • When you hear the horn, hide: the waiting room, or tight behind a pillar.
  • 6 torn diary pages (Maa's) are hidden across the chapters, some only in torchlight. Her 6 lost things turn up for Lost & Found, and Room 0 is locked with a code.
  • 3 endings: get through all three chapters; find every diary page for Maa; pages, her things and Room 0 for The 0217.
  • Press V (phone: 🎥) to switch between first- and third-person view.

PC: WASD / arrows to walk, mouse to look, Shift to run, E to use / pick up, F torch, Q / right-click torch flare, R / left-click swing, Space shove, Esc to pause.
Phone: left thumb to walk (push far to run), right thumb to look, USE, 🔦 torch and 🔆 flare buttons on the right, plus ⚔️ swing and ✋ shove when you need them (holding something: drag to turn it, or USE). Play in landscape.
Progress is saved at the start of each chapter.

THE DAILY LOCAL
MUMBAI · MONDAY, 31 OCTOBER 2016 · ₹3

Commuter found on disused "Platform 0" at Shunya Road

A young commuter was struck by a passing goods train late on Sunday night on the old Platform 0 at Shunya Road station, which railway officials say has been closed to passengers for years. He was wearing headphones and carrying a red backpack. Witnesses said he had been waiting for the 11:58 slow local, which was cancelled that night. A chai vendor told police the young man "kept walking up and down the platform, as if he had lost something." His ticket was found on a bench. Railway police have appealed to anyone who can identify him. Officials say the platform will be sealed. Regular commuters say they still see someone there, every night, a little before midnight.
